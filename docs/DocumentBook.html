ingestFraudProof(body, ctxopt) → {Object}

Verify a `FraudProof` body (`types/witness`) and penalize the offender locally. The penalty lands on the offender's x-only key and sticks: every seller whose offers carry that key as their verified AMP signer (`ampSignerPubkey`, from `signerPubkeyHex` at ingest) scores with it, now and on later ingests. Self-asserted `sellerPubkey` / `sellerId` never route a penalty. Each evidence id counts once; verdicts that name no offender (`reputational: false`) penalize nobody. Reputation stays local (decision 4).
