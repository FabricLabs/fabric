fill(side, amountSats, optsopt) → {Object}

Walk one side of the book for `amountSats` of L1, best price first, then earliest order. Each fill respects the order's `minSats` / `maxSats`; an order whose minimum exceeds what is left, or whose fee leaves nothing to pay on L2 for that amount, is skipped. Nothing is reserved — the taker settles each fill with its maker.
