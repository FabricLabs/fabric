Class: ChannelSession

ChannelSession()

Off-chain state of one Fabric payment channel from the local party's perspective: balances, pending HTLCs (one per chunk), each party's current commitment and the counterparty's signature on **ours**. Updates are exchanged as signed `CONTRACT_MESSAGE` bodies (Channel.BODY_TYPES): 1. proposer → `ChannelCommitment n` (signature on the *counterparty's* commitment `n`, next per-commitment point); 2. counterparty → `ChannelCommitment n` reply (signature on the proposer's commitment `n`, inline `revoke` of its own state `n-1`, next point); 3. proposer → `ChannelRevoke n-1`. A party that broadcasts a revoked commitment loses everything on it (Channel.prepareJusticeSpend); the latest commitment pays the holder only after `toSelfDelay`. Open is three messages (`ChannelOpen`, reply, ack) because the funder cannot sign the counterparty's commitment before learning its first per-commitment point. Both parties proposing state `n` at once is resolved deterministically: the funder's proposal wins, the non-funder rolls its own back and replays it (ChannelSession#repropose) once the round completes. After a reconnect, ChannelSession#reestablish tells the peer which states we hold signed and revoked; the answer resends whatever we missed.

fulfillHtlc(id, preimageHex) → {object}

Settle an HTLC with its preimage: the amount moves to the **receiver's** balance and the proposal carries the preimage. Normally the receiver reveals; the sender may also settle an HTLC it offered once it learned the preimage elsewhere (e.g. the seller's public key reveal) — that only ever pays the counterparty. The receiver reveals only while ChannelSession#canFulfil: once the preimage is out, a sender that withholds its countersignature forces the receiver on-chain, where the receiver's claim is CSV-delayed by `toSelfDelay` on its own commitment. The sender's timeout there waits `toSelfDelay + htlcClaimWindowBlocks`, and on the sender's commitment the receiver claims immediately, so a fulfill with that margin cannot be raced. Too close to expiry the preimage is only remembered and the watchdog (ChannelSession#tick) asks the host to claim on-chain.

tick(tipHeightopt) → {Object}

Chain-time watchdog. Polls funding confirmations, settles what it can off-chain (fulfill received HTLCs whose preimage is known while the margin allows; fail expired received HTLCs without one; fail expired offered HTLCs) and returns the on-chain **actions** the host must take: - `{ type: 'forceClose', channelId, reason: 'channel-expiry'|'htlc-claim'|'htlc-timeout', deadline, tip }` — at `expireAfter − toSelfDelay − closeMarginBlocks` for the channel, when a received HTLC with a known preimage can no longer be fulfilled off-chain, or `htlcSafetyBlocks` after an offered HTLC expired unresolved; - `{ type: 'claimHtlc', channelId, htlcId, paymentHashHex, preimageHex, amountSats, expireAfter, deadline }` (Channel.prepareHtlcClaim); - `{ type: 'timeoutHtlc', channelId, htlcId, paymentHashHex, amountSats, expireAfter }` (Channel.prepareHtlcTimeout). HTLCs are taken from the current state and the last countersigned one, so an HTLC removed by a round still in flight is watched until it completes.
