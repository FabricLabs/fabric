(protected) _beforeInsert(entry)

A maker at its entry limit loses its oldest entry. In a full book the maker holding the most entries (counting the incoming one; the incoming maker on a tie) gives up its oldest, so one key cannot flush every other maker out; with no such maker the book's oldest row goes.
