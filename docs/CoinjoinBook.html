choose(amountSats, count, optsopt) → {Array.<Object>}

Draw up to `count` distinct makers for a coinjoin of `amountSats`, at random without replacement, weighted by bond value. Unbonded makers weigh `noBondShare` × the mean bond value (or all weigh 1 when nobody is bonded). Fees above `maxFeeSats` are excluded, never ranked.

(async) refreshBonds(verifier) → {Promise.<{checked: number, valid: number, shared: number}>}

Check every advertised bond on chain. A maker's value is its best valid bond; an outpoint whose bond key certified more than one maker counts for none. Bond descriptors are public, so only a claim that verifies on chain (certificate, output script, unspent) can share an outpoint: a copy, or a certificate from a key that does not lock the output, leaves the owner's bond standing.
