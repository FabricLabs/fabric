Class: ChannelBook

ChannelBook()

Local registry of ChannelSessions for a host (Hub, GoonCitizen, Passport): looks a channel up by counterparty, routes inbound `Channel*` `CONTRACT_MESSAGE` bodies to the right session, forwards multi-hop HTLCs, ticks sessions on new chain tips, and returns the bodies and on-chain actions the host must handle. Shape expected by the market agent's `attach({ channels })`. **Seller-fulfills (Lightning shape):** when the host supplies `preimageFor(paymentHashHex, htlc)` the book fulfills every received HTLC the host releases a preimage for as soon as the round that committed it completes; the payer learns the preimage from the resulting `ChannelHtlcFulfill`. The host sees the HTLC (amount, expiry, blob hash) and must refuse underpaid ones. **Pay-after-verify credit:** a seller may serve a chunk before payment, up to `creditLimitSats` outstanding per channel (ChannelBook#extendCredit); the buyer pays with a balance transfer (ChannelBook#transfer) whose `ref` names the credit. No HTLC, so no HTLC minimum. Events: `htlcCommitted` (a received HTLC is irrevocably committed), `htlcFulfilled` (a counterparty revealed a preimage to us), `htlcSettled` (any HTLC left the channel; `outcome` is `fulfilled` or `failed`), `transferReceived` (a balance transfer to us is final: the payer revoked the state before it), `creditExtended` / `creditSettled`, and `channelAction` (a new watchdog action, see ChannelSession#tick). HTLC payloads are `{ channelId, session, htlcId, paymentHashHex, blobHashHex, amountSats, direction, preimageHex?, outcome?, reason? }`.

extendCredit(opts) → {Object}

Seller side of pay-after-verify: serve first, record that the buyer owes `amountSats` for `ref` over its channel with us. Refused without an open channel, for a `ref` already used, past the credit limit, or when the buyer's channel balance (above its reserve) could not pay everything it would then owe. Paid by a transfer carrying the same `ref`. Also takes the request `Peer` passes to `settings.sealedBlobCredit` (`buyer`, `buyerPubkey`, `priceSats`), so hosts wire `sealedBlobCredit: (r) => book.extendCredit(r)`.
