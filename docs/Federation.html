(static) settlePegOutPayout(content, requestId, opts) → {Object|Object}

After L1 vault payout confirms, settle a pending burn: drop pendingBurns and vaultConfirmed by the same amountSats (request-scoped). The caller verifies the payout on L1; this helper only settles against a named payout output that matches the burned withdrawal exactly, and never lets one output settle two withdrawals. Fees: the payout output must carry the full withdrawal `amountSats`; the federation pays the L1 fee from vault funds above `outstandingSats + pendingBurnsSats`. Without `opts.vaultConfirmedSats` the vault only drops by `amountSats`, so the fee stays counted in it until the caller passes the verified vault total (which fails conservation when there was no surplus to pay the fee from). A settled payout that later leaves the best chain is undone with Federation.unsettlePegOutPayout; wait for `payoutMinConfirmations` to make that rare.
